    Tracking tips
    • Cyan means the color matches blood. Red sumac, dogwood and maple leaves can also light up, so check what's highlighted.
    • Bright red blood with bubbles points to a lung hit. Dark red can mean liver. Green or brown flecks mean a gut shot, so back out and wait.
    • Use Dried for sign that's turned brown after a few hours, and raise sensitivity in low light.
    • Tap the viewfinder to freeze a frame and look closer.
    • Check the undersides of leaves and grass about knee to waist high.

    Getting your property lines
    1. Open Google Earth (web or desktop) and find your land.
    2. Use Add path or polygon and click around your lines. Most county parcel viewers show the lines so you can trace them.
    3. Save or export the shape as a KML file, then import it here.
    4. Phone GPS is usually accurate to about 5–15 yards under trees, so treat anything close to the line as "on the line."
    5. Never cross onto a neighbor's land to recover a deer without their permission.
