Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Blood Trail Finder ("the App") is a free camera-based tool for tracking deer blood trails while hunting. Your privacy matters — here is exactly what the App does and does not do with your data.

What data the App uses

  • Camera access — The App uses your phone's camera to display a live viewfinder and run on-device color analysis. Camera frames are processed in real time on your phone and are never transmitted, uploaded, or stored beyond the current session.
  • Location (GPS) — If you grant permission, the App reads your GPS coordinates to calculate sunrise/sunset times, display your position on the map, mark blood spots, and check property boundaries. Location data is stored only on your phone in your browser's local storage.
  • Photos — If you use the "Take photo" feature, the image is analyzed on your phone and is not uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • Local storage — The App saves your settings, trail log, and property boundary on your phone using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

What the App does NOT do

  • Does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers
  • Does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • Does not share any data with third parties
  • Does not require an account or sign-in
  • Does not access your contacts, microphone, or files beyond photos you choose to open

Third-party services

The App loads map tiles from Esri, OpenStreetMap, and USGS when you use the map view. These services may log standard web request data (your IP address) according to their own privacy policies. No personal information from the App is sent to them.

Data deletion

All App data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this app, or by uninstalling the app. There is nothing to delete on our end because we don't have your data.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to the developer through the app's Google Play Store listing.
